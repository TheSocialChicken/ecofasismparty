## 3. Shot-by-Shot Timeline (For Multi-Scene Video Editors)

### Shot 1: The Intake Checkpoint (Opening)
```text
Slow tracking dolly shot through a damp, foggy concrete security checkpoint. Austere officers in dark wool uniforms examine vintage paper passports under harsh fluorescent lamps. Camera pushes in on a heavy mechanical rubber stamp slamming down in slow motion, leaving a crisp black seal reading "MINISERVICE". Cinematic 35mm film, hyperrealistic, shallow depth of field, rain droplets on glass.
```

### Shot 2: The Bicycle Dynamo Grid (Labor Shift)
```text
Low-angle tracking shot through a vast brutalist warehouse cellar. Dozens of silent citizens intensely pedaling stationary copper bicycle dynamos. Thick black power cables snake across damp cobblestones toward a humongous flickering carbon LED score counter displaying red numbers. Amber sparks, volumetric dust motes, moody atmospheric lighting.
```

### Shot 3: The Resistance Cellar (Infiltration)
```text
Over-the-shoulder medium shot inside a clandestine underground server room lit by neon ultraviolet and amber tubes. A hooded resistance operative imprints a glowing cracked stag icon onto another operative's ankle. Server racks glow in the background as green terminal text scrolls rapidly on CRT monitors. Gritty cyberpunk espionage, filmic shadows.
```

### Shot 4: The 00:05 Revolution (Climax)
```text
Epic slow-motion high-angle shot in a crowded, opulent banquet hall lit by candlelight and green neon. Scores of guests in eco-formal velvet attire throw shredded paper debt certificates into the air like white confetti. Alarm strobes pulse deep red through the falling paper blizzard. Cinematic masterpiece, dramatic motion blur, emotional uprising.
```
