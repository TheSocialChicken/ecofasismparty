Epoch I — The Great Acceleration
~2025 – 2045: The Compute Wars

In the desperate scramble to scale synthetic artificial intelligence, multinational tech conglomerates purchased dedicated baseload nuclear power plants — Three Mile Island, small modular reactors — and devoured electrical grids to power gigawatt-scale server clusters. While the general public faced rolling green blackouts, sky-high tariffs, and austerity, hyperscalers drained freshwater watersheds to cool computing clusters. It was the age of digital excess, frantic automation, and atmospheric exhaustion.

Epoch II — The Great Withering
~2045 – 2080: The Long Collapse

Humanity did not perish in a single evening. Over four grueling decades of cascading agricultural failures, thermal river collapses, and nuclear-containment breaches during extreme European heatwaves, 95% of human civilization withered away. Food distribution collapsed, cities emptied, and the surviving corporate conglomerates fused with militant ecological sects to seize the remaining habitable biomes.

The Core Premise

The outside world suffered civilizational collapse. Somewhere in the ruined, scorched wasteland of Europe, word spread of one place that still functions: Ecotopia, the last sustainable sanctuary. Citizens apply through a digital portal to enter its borders.

What they discover is that Ecotopia is a totalitarian ecological regime governed by R.E.I.G.N. — the Regime for Ecological Integrity and Global Nurturing. Corporations and ecologist-theocrats have fused into a paternalistic lockdown state. Tonally, it is Orwell's 1984, but the enemy is not a foreign superpower; the enemy is ecological collapse itself.

The golden law of the masquerade: Ecotopia is never referred to as cruel or dystopian in-character. Every horrifying act of authoritarian control is masked behind virtuous, loving, maternal bureaucracy. It is the calm, chilling conviction of an administration that believes absolute coercion is the highest form of ecological love.
