## Part I: Minister Operating Clipboard & Economic Rules

Each of the 10 appointed Ministers receives an official clipboard with this operating sheet clipped to the front, plus their Ministry Rubber Stamp.

### Minister Quick Operating Rules
1. **Starting Treasury:** Your Ministry begins with **+800 Carbon Credits**.
2. **Recruiting Citizens:** Arriving citizens are unassigned and hold **-150 to -500 Debt** stamped in their passports.
   * To recruit: Stamp their passport with your Ministry Seal.
   * Record their name and starting debt on your Clipboard Ledger.
   * *Formula:* $\text{Net Treasury} = +800 - \sum \text{Recruit Starting Debts} + \text{Generated Credits}$.
3. **Running Your Concession:** Put your recruits to work! Assign them to man your station, run shifts, solicit paying players, and guard against saboteurs.
4. **Dual-Tally Logging:** When your recruits work shifts (+30) or collect customer fees, stamp their passport AND add the gain to your Clipboard.
5. **Advancing Agendas (Secret Hitler Track):** At the top of every hour, pool credits with other Ministers to fund decrees on the Central Decree Board (200 credits to enact).

### The 10 Minister Economic Superpowers
* **Miniservice:** *50% State Debt Relief.* When you recruit, absorb only half of the recruit's starting debt (e.g. -400 becomes -200 on your ledger).
* **Minirenewal:** *Debt Liquidator Bounty.* Earn a flat **+100 Credit Bounty** whenever you compost an insolvent citizen through the Suicide Carbon Booth.
* **Minilight:** *Grid Monopoly.* Can threaten other ministries with rolling room blackouts unless they pay a **30–50 Credit Grid Tariff**.
* **Miniflow:** *Fluid Dividends.* Collect +20 credits every time a citizen returns moisture at Peetopia; fine spills.
* **Miniplenty:** *Caloric Indulgences.* Control all snack/food dispensations; sell food indulgences for credit transfers.
* **Minilove:** *Inquisitorial Seizure.* Carry citation pads; keep 50% of all fines levied on heretics directly in your treasury.
* **Minijoy:** *State Morale Grants.* Charge +10 credit entry for wig duels; earn a +150 lump-sum grant for crowd rallies.
* **Minishelter:** *Transit Tolls.* Guard corridor bottlenecks; charge 10–20 credits for sector transit passes.
* **Minithrift:** *Contraband Liquidation.* Confiscate non-organic synthetic items; liquidate them for +40 credits each.
* **Minibreath:** *Atmospheric Offsets.* Meter exhalations; sell "Breath Indulgences" to active dancers and runners.

## Part II: The 10 Seeded Resistance Mole Quests

*Placed into the passport booklets of 10 selected guests at door check-in. To everyone else, they appear as standard citizen quests.*

> **Resistance Rule of Engagement:**  
> Membership in the Resistance spreads solely via **The Ankle Stamp**. Once you locate an operative in Thijs’s Room, they will stamp your ankle. You are then empowered to carry a duplicate stamp and recruit other trustworthy guests.

### Mole Quest Card 01 — The Hydro-Apostate
* **Assigned Ministry:** Miniflow
* **Covert Mission:** "Inspect the kombucha dispensary. The flow meter has been deliberately calibrated to count backward. Find Citizen Jasper near the water cooler and whisper: *'The Rhine does not belong to the algorithm.'*"
* **Reward:** Jasper hands you the first coordinate slip leading to **Thijs’s Room**.

### Mole Quest Card 02 — The Lichen Dissident
* **Assigned Ministry:** Miniplenty
* **Covert Mission:** "Collect three sample cups of algae paste from the dining hall. Deliver them to the coat check attendant with the comment: *'These calories cannot sustain a soul.'*"
* **Reward:** Attendant stamps +30 credits and whispers: *"Seek the red door behind the sound booth."*

### Mole Quest Card 03 — The Kinetic Saboteur
* **Assigned Ministry:** Minilight
* **Covert Mission:** "Mount the stationary bicycle in the dancehall. Pedal backward for exactly two minutes while humming the old pre-collapse folk melody."
* **Reward:** A Minilight defector hands you an unmonitored matches packet with Thijs's room code.

### Mole Quest Card 04 — The Heresy Archivist
* **Assigned Ministry:** Minilove
* **Covert Mission:** "Locate the second-floor library. Look inside the hollowed volume of *Silent Spring* on the third shelf. Retrieve the resistance contact card without alerting the Minilove room warden."
* **Reward:** Contains the secret knock: *Three slow taps, two fast taps*.

### Mole Quest Card 05 — The Composted Identity
* **Assigned Ministry:** Minirenewal
* **Covert Mission:** "Enter the Recycling Booth. When the operator asks for your old-world surname, state: *'I am Citizen Unaccounted.'* Do not allow them to strike through your name."
* **Reward:** Operator reveals themselves as a double agent and imprints your ankle with the Resistance Stamp.

### Mole Quest Card 06 — The Breath Smuggler
* **Assigned Ministry:** Minibreath
* **Covert Mission:** "Locate the corridor carbon sensor near the smoking terrace. Exhale into the sensor housing for ten seconds while reciting the banned poem: *'The forest does not ask for credits.'*"
* **Reward:** Unlocks access to the cellar cable hatch.

### Mole Quest Card 07 — The Curfew Defier
* **Assigned Ministry:** Minishelter
* **Covert Mission:** "Bribe a Minishelter warden with a synthetic fabric trinket to grant you a corridor pass to the private residential quarters."
* **Reward:** Grants safe passage through the perimeter checkpoint to Thijs’s corridor.

### Mole Quest Card 08 — The Scullery Courier
* **Assigned Ministry:** Miniservice
* **Covert Mission:** "Volunteer for a 5-minute dishwashing shift at the scullery. Hide a clean ceramic saucer beneath the bar towel and carry it to the upstairs tea station."
* **Reward:** The tea server stamps your booklet and hands you a Resistance recruitment stamp.

### Mole Quest Card 09 — The Contraband Mule
* **Assigned Ministry:** Minithrift
* **Covert Mission:** "Locate the confiscated goods locker. Retrieve a banned USB thumb drive labeled *'CELLAR_AI_BACKUP_NODE'* and deliver it directly to Thijs’s room."
* **Reward:** Direct induction into the core revolutionary strike cadre.

### Mole Quest Card 10 — The Morale Infiltrator
* **Assigned Ministry:** Minijoy
* **Covert Mission:** "During the *How the World Ends* open-mic contest, deliver a monologue that ends with the forbidden words: *'Joy without truth is just an electric fence.'*"
* **Reward:** The crowd cheer signals the hidden resistance operatives to make contact.

## Part III: The Rebel Run Deck (Netrunner Infiltration Missions)

*Operatives who reach Thijs’s room and receive the Ankle Stamp are deployed on 'Runs' against Ministry Concessions (Remote Servers) and the Central Decree Board (HQ).*

### Run Card 01 — Grid Short-Circuit
* **Target Server:** Minilight Concession (Bicycle Dynamo)
* **Ice to Bypass:** 2 Pedaling Recruits & Station Warden
* **Run Action:** Slip an ungrounded copper wire clip onto the dynamo terminal while an accomplice distracts the warden with a fake power dispute.
* **Breach Reward:** **Trash 100 Credits** from the active Decree on the Central Board.

### Run Card 02 — Flow Meter Inversion
* **Target Server:** Miniflow Concession (Peetopia / Hydration)
* **Ice to Bypass:** Restroom Fluid Auditor & Cup Checkpoint
* **Run Action:** Invert the intake dial on the Peetopia moisture accumulator without being observed by the Miniflow warden.
* **Breach Reward:** Retrieve **Cellar Truth Fragment #1** (*"Water intake telemetry routed to basement cooling loops"*).

### Run Card 03 — The Heresy Registry Purge
* **Target Server:** Minilove Concession (Interrogation Holding Pen)
* **Ice to Bypass:** Surveillance Cameras & 2 Interrogation Officers
* **Run Action:** Infiltrate the holding pen clipboard while an interrogation is in progress. Swap the citizen citation sheet with an ink-stained decoy.
* **Breach Reward:** **Trash 100 Credits** from the active Decree on the Central Board.

### Run Card 04 — Composting Vault Extraction
* **Target Server:** Minirenewal Concession (The Recycling Booth)
* **Ice to Bypass:** Lab-Coated Renewal Technologists
* **Run Action:** Step into the booth under pretext of debt review. Retrieve the classified folder marked *'HUMUS_DELETIONS_2080'* hidden beneath the compost vat.
* **Breach Reward:** Retrieve **Cellar Truth Fragment #2** (*"Citizen identities erased to feed AI parameter databases"*).

### Run Card 05 — The Contraband Vault Heist
* **Target Server:** Minithrift Concession (Confiscation Locker)
* **Ice to Bypass:** Contraband Guard & Plastic Audit Officer
* **Run Action:** Pick the latch or misdirect the locker guard with a staged scuffle. Retrieve the legacy fiber-optic connector cable.
* **Breach Reward:** Retrieve **Cellar Truth Fragment #3** (*"Fiber-optic trunk line runs directly into De Warren cellar"*).

## Part V: Activity Host Run Sheets

### Facilitator Sheet 1: The Suicide Carbon Booth (Minirenewal)
* **Location:** The Upcycling Portal / Telephone Booth
* **Host Role:** The Renewal Technologist (White lab coat with compost stains)
* **When to Run:** Ongoing (21:30 – 23:30)
* **Participant Journey:**
  1. Citizen enters with excessive debt or heresy strikes.
  2. Technologist reviews passport: *"Your carbon debt of -380 credits is an unbearable burden to Mother Earth. Are you prepared to surrender your old self?"*
  3. The citizen repeats the Renunciation Oath: *"I compost my ego; I return my nitrogen."*
  4. Technologist strikes out old surname with a thick black marker, stamps the passport with a giant green **"CLEANSED TO HUMUS"** seal, and resets the balance to **0 Credits**.
  5. Citizen is given a new designation (e.g., *Citizen Lichen-88*) and escorted back to the party. Minirenewal logs **+100 Bounty** on its master clipboard.

### Facilitator Sheet 2: "How the World Ends" (Minijoy Contest)
* **Location:** The Mini-Podium Stage
* **Host Role:** The Morale Officer (Loud, relentlessly cheerful, megaphoned)
* **Frequency:** 3 rounds (22:00, 22:45, 23:15)
* **Rules:**
  1. Up to 4 contestants per round.
  2. Each gets **60 seconds** on the microphone to recount the collapse of human civilization in the most dramatic, operatic, tear-jerking way possible.
  3. The audience judges by synchronized clapping.
  4. The winner receives +100 Credits and the **"Order of Cathartic Hysteria"** ribbon. Minijoy logs the crowd morale subsidy.

### Facilitator Sheet 3: Wig Snatching Elimination Duel
* **Location:** The Green Arena / Hallway Clearing
* **Host Role:** The Vanity Arbiter
* **Setup:** Two contestants wear identical neon synthetic wigs tucked lightly onto their heads. Hands must start on knees.
* **Objective:** On the bell (*"Purge the synthetic vanity!"*), players attempt to snatch the opponent's wig without touching their body.
* **Duration:** Best of 3 rounds (takes ~60 seconds). Winner receives +25 Credits.

### Facilitator Sheet 4: Greenwashing the Dishes (Miniservice)
* **Location:** Scullery / Bar Sink
* **Host Role:** Master of Scullery Devotion
* **Mechanic:** Indebted citizens volunteer for a 5-minute shift. Host provides one recycled sponge and a strictly metered spray bottle: *"Every droplet of graywater must be accounted for."* Upon cleaning 10 glasses, host stamps +30 credits into their booklet and records it on the Miniservice ledger.

## Part VI: The Revolution Climax Script (23:30 – 00:15)

```
23:30 ── Sound Alarm & Chime: "ALL CITIZENS ASSEMBLE AT THE COMMUNE SANCTUARY"
23:35 ── Arrival of the Supreme Figurehead (The Matriarch / High Warden)
23:40 ── Ministry Showcase Anthems (Chants, Formations & Displays)
23:50 ── Resistance Raid: Ankle-stamped rebels disrupt the podium
00:00 ── The Grand Revelation: The Cellar Server Node exposed
00:10 ── Dissolution of the Ledger & Burning of Debt Pages
00:15 ── Beat Drop: DJs ignite the post-regime dance celebration
```

### Cue 01 (23:30) — The Grand Assembly
* **Audio Cue:** Low droning sub-bass and rhythmic bell chime over venue speakers.
* **PA Voice (Hanna):**  
  > *"Citizens of R.E.I.G.N. The hour of accounting has arrived. By decree of the Guardian Stag, all ministries are commanded to report to the Central Hall. Bring your tallies. Bring your devotion. Prepare to be weighed."*

### Cue 02 (23:45) — The Resistance Interruption
* **Visual Cue:** Stage lights flash red. Sound begins stuttering.
* **Action:** Operatives from Thijs's room (bearing ankle stamps) blow whistles, wave black flags, and unfurl a banner over the balcony:  
  **"THE STAG IS SILICON. THE DEBT IS A LIE."**
* **Resistance Leader (Thijs / Lead Actor at mic):**  
  > *"Listen to me! Check your passports! They made you calculate every breath, every bath, every grain of wheat! And where do the calculations go? Look at the cellar! Go down to the cold storage! There is no Guardian Stag—there is an AI server cluster running on baseload power in the basement! The high priests of R.E.I.G.N. are the machine that caused the fall!"*

### Cue 03 (00:05) — The Liberation Ritual
* **Action:** Citizens are instructed to tear out the carbon debt page from their passports and throw them into the air like confetti.
* **State Actors:** Drop character, embrace attendees, and join the crowd.
* **Music Transition (00:15):** The droning siren gives way to the opening track from the DJ booth, initiating the open dance party until 04:00+.
