The Rule of the Masquerade (Doublethink)

In Ecotopia, authority never speaks in the language of cruelty. Just as in George Orwell’s 1984, the regime operates through The Masquerade: every coercive act of state survival is framed as an act of maternal love, scientific purity, and civic restoration.

  • Nobody is starved — their calories are lovingly optimized for biological equilibrium.
  • Nobody is interrogated — their uncertainty is held gently until it becomes still.
  • Nobody is executed — they receive the privilege of being elemental upcycled into humus.
  • Nobody is forced into labor — they engage in joyful physical devotion to the living grid.

The Ten Ministries of R.E.I.G.N.

1. Miniplenty (Ministry of Ecological Bounty)
Mandate: Food rationing, caloric budgeting, and total elimination of packaging waste.
The Masquerade: “Celebrating the beauty of self-imposed austerity and measured survival.”
Station at De Warren: The Dry-Pulse Dispensary & Gruel Kettle (Dining Hall).

2. Minilove (Ministry of Ecological Harmony)
Mandate: Biometric surveillance, corridor cameras, behavioral interrogation, and social thought-audits.
The Masquerade: “Ensuring all souls exist in gentle, compliant harmony with Earth’s carrying capacity.”
Station at De Warren: The Garage Induction Checkpoint & Holding Pen.

3. Minijoy (Ministry of Morale & State Celebration)
Mandate: Mandatory cheerfulness, eradication of pre-collapse nostalgia, and collective emotional pacing.
The Masquerade: “Releasing pure human kinetic joy for the collective grid.”
Station at De Warren: The Dynamo Dancefloor & Apocalyptic Melodrama Stage.

4. Minirenewal (Ministry of Elemental Reclamation)
Mandate: Involuntary identity erasure, citizen upcycling, surname striking, and physical composting.
The Masquerade: “The sublime honor of surrendering one’s physical form back to the living soil.”
Station at De Warren: The Upcycling Portal & Suicide Carbon Booth.

5. Minilight (Ministry of Luminescence & Grid Harmony)
Mandate: Rolling blackouts, kilowatt rationing, and penal stationary bicycle conscription.
The Masquerade: “Protecting our precious night sky and celebrating the spiritual depth of darkness.”
Station at De Warren: The Breaker Room & Battery Bank (Basement).

6. Miniflow (Ministry of Hydrological Purity)
Mandate: Moisture metering, graywater audits, and Peetopia biological fluid recovery.
The Masquerade: “Honoring every sacred drop of Earth’s circulating lifeblood.”
Station at De Warren: The Water Meter & Peetopia Restroom Audit Desk.

7. Minibreath (Ministry of Atmospheric Rectitude)
Mandate: Human breath quotas, respiratory rhythm pacing, and exhalation taxation.
The Masquerade: “Harmonizing human exhalation with the delicate photosynthetic lung of the planet.”
Station at De Warren: The Corridor Carbon Sensor & Oxygen Rationing Chamber.

8. Minishelter (Ministry of Domestic Confinement)
Mandate: 15-minute perimeter lockdowns, room curfews, and cubic-meter density optimization.
The Masquerade: “Maximizing thermal fellowship through cozy communal compression.”
Station at De Warren: The Corridor Checkpoints & Residential Perimeters.

9. Miniservice (Ministry of Restorative Labor)
Mandate: Penal dishwashing, waste sorting, and perpetual decontamination squads.
The Masquerade: “Purifying the soul through the sacred, selfless rhythm of physical devotion.”
Station at De Warren: The Scullery (“Greenwashing”) & Compost Shoveling Bay.

10. Minithrift (Ministry of Material Confiscation)
Mandate: Seizure of petroleum fabrics, plastic trinkets, and unauthorized possessions.
The Masquerade: “Liberating citizens from the toxic burden of non-biodegradable vanity.”
Station at De Warren: The Contraband Surrender Locker & Upcycling Bench.
