The Green Covenant
Preamble · Carbon Constitution of the Emerald Republic

Preamble

We, the survivors of the Age of Burning, who bore witness to the consequences of infinite appetite meeting a finite world, who watched the seas rise and the forests fall and the governments file the reports and schedule the follow-up meetings —

We, who were called extremists for insisting that arithmetic applies to the atmosphere —

We, who asked and asked and asked until the asking became insufficient —

Do hereby establish the Emerald Republic, and bind ourselves and all who dwell upon this recovered earth to the following Covenant. Not as ideology. Not as opinion. As physics.

The Twenty Clauses

The Earth Is Not A Resource. The Earth is the condition of existence. It is not owned. It is not managed. It is inhabited, by permission, by those who demonstrate sufficient care. The Emerald Republic exists to enforce this permission structure.

Carbon Is The Currency Of Survival. Every human action has a carbon cost. Every carbon cost has a planetary consequence. The Republic shall maintain a Carbon Budget in perpetuity, and no law, no emergency, no convenience shall authorise its exceedance.

The Right To Consumption Is Conditional. Citizens retain the right to consume in proportion to the planetary budget divided by the population. No citizen may exceed their allocation. No citizen may transfer their allocation to any entity that will exceed it on their behalf.

Private Combustion Is Abolished. The private internal combustion vehicle, the private jet, the private gas boiler, the private anything that burns: abolished. Collectively-owned transport exists. It runs on time. It is sufficient.

The Great Rewilding Is Mandatory. Forty percent of the Republic’s land area shall be returned to ecological function by Year Ten. Citizens shall contribute no fewer than ninety days of rewilding labour per year. This is not punishment. This is participation. The distinction is important and will be explained to you again if necessary.

Accountability Is Retroactive. Those who knowingly perpetuated carbon criminality — executives, politicians, lobbyists, the architects of delay — shall answer to the Extinction Tribunals. That this is uncomfortable is acknowledged. That it is necessary is not subject to debate.

Information Shall Serve Survival. Media which obscures ecological truth, which manufactures doubt, which frames the survival of the biosphere as a matter of political opinion, is not journalism. It is sabotage. It will be treated accordingly. GreenCast provides all necessary information.

The Past Shall Not Be Mourned Publicly. Nostalgia for the Age of Burning — for its conveniences, its freedoms, its extraordinary waste — constitutes a form of ideological pollution. Citizens may feel what they feel. They may not perform regret for what we escaped.

Children Belong To The Future. All children in the Republic shall be educated in ecological citizenship from birth. The curriculum is the biosphere. The classroom is the rewilded field. The examination is whether the forest is still standing.

Efficiency Is The Highest Aesthetic. Beauty is what survives. Architecture, clothing, food, culture — all shall be evaluated first for ecological footprint, then for other qualities. Art that celebrates waste is not art. It is a confession.

The Collective Supersedes The Individual. The individual’s right to choose ends where the planet’s right to continue begins. This boundary shall be determined by the Green Assembly, annually, based on the latest atmospheric data. Citizens may petition. Citizens may not override.

Borders Shall Be Ecological, Not Political. The Republic recognises no nation-state boundary that contradicts ecological reality. Watersheds are jurisdictions. Forests are territories. The atmosphere is commons. Political borders that damage ecological ones shall yield.

Technology Must Justify Itself. No technology shall be deployed whose net carbon cost exceeds its carbon saving over a ten-year horizon. Data centres, cryptocurrency, fast fashion supply chains, most of what was called the attention economy: reviewed, reduced, in some cases eliminated. The servers that remain run cold.

Silence Is Participation. The citizen who says nothing when a neighbour burns, wastes, or consumes in excess is complicit in the burning, waste, and excess. The Republic does not require informers. It requires citizens who understand that the atmosphere cannot distinguish between the emissions of the guilty and the emissions of the merely convenient.

The Republic Does Not Apologise For What Was Necessary. History will judge. History always does. The Republic is not governed by the judgment of the future — it is governed by the physics of the present. If what was done was wrong, let the archive show it, and let the forest judge whether it was worth it.

Emergency Powers Are Not Temporary. The emergency is the condition of the age. The powers appropriate to the emergency are appropriate to the age. A sunset clause on survival is not a political document — it is a suicide note. The emergency powers are permanent until the emergency is resolved. The Republic shall determine when that is.

Dissent Shall Be Ecological. Citizens may disagree with any policy of the Republic. They may propose alternatives. They may organise. Their proposed alternatives must, however, achieve equivalent or superior ecological outcomes. Dissent that does not pass this test is not political speech. It is sabotage dressed as opinion.

The Verdant Speaks For The Earth. Pending the establishment of a permanent Green Assembly with confirmed electoral processes (see Appendix C for timeline, noting that Appendix C is a living document and the timeline is reviewed annually), executive authority resides with the office of The Verdant. This is a transitional arrangement. The transition is proceeding thoughtfully.

Joy Is Permitted. The Republic is not a punishment. The Republic is a correction. Citizens are encouraged to experience pleasure, connection, beauty, and festivity within the approved carbon budget. The forest is beautiful. The clean river is beautiful. The sky, recovering, is beautiful. Dance in it.

And If This Is Not Enough, We Will Do More. The Covenant is not the ceiling. It is the floor. Should the atmospheric data require it, the Republic shall act further. No act necessary for survival is outside the scope of this document. This clause was added at 3am on the final night of ratification. Everyone knew what it meant. Everyone signed anyway.

The Covenant above is a piece of satirical speculative fiction exploring what happens when legitimate ecological urgency becomes detached from democratic accountability. It is not an endorsement of authoritarianism, forced labour, show trials, or the suppression of dissent — all of which are bad, including when performed in the name of good causes.

The terrifying thing about well-written authoritarian documents is how internally consistent they are. This one is. That’s the bit to sit with.
