7. FACILITATOR PRINCIPLES
Rules of Engagement for Activity Creators & Ministers
1. Always Word Positively: Punishments are "privileges"; starvation is "caloric purity"; forced chores are "joyful devotion."

2. Low Barrier to Entry: Games must take under 3 minutes to explain and under 10 minutes to play.

3. Award Credit Stamps: Every completed shift or quest must offer an immediate physical passport stamp (+10 to +50 CC) and a clipboard tally.

5. LEGISLATIVE COUNCIL & MOLES
Secret Hitler Dynamics • Subversive Procedures
• The Council: Every 45 minutes, High Warden convenes the 10 Ministers. Two decree cards are drawn from the state deck.

COUNCIL RESOLUTION: A passed subversive decree freezes all Agendas for 20 minutes and unlocks safe passage through corridor gates.

2. CARBON INSOLVENCY
Default Penalties, Defections & Composting
• Insolvency (Net Balance < 0): If a Ministry recklessly hires too many unproductive debtors, its net balance drops below zero.

• Insolvency Penalties: VIP Room access frozen for all members, aggressive Minilove audits, voting rights stripped.

• Defections & Liquidation: Insolvent Ministries can sell high-performing recruits to rivals or dump unproductive debtors at Minirenewal to compost their contract for +100 bounty.
