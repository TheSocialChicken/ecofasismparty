THE TEN MINISTRIES OF R.E.I.G.N.

Upon arrival, every citizen is drafted into one of the Ten Newspeak Ministries to perform joyful civic restitution:

1. Miniplenty

Caloric Bounty & Rations: Algae paste, steamed pulses, and zero-waste rationing. The ration has been lovingly optimized.

2. Minilove

Ecological Harmony: Total biometric surveillance, thought-audits, and gentle ideological re-alignment holding pens.

3. Minijoy

Mandatory Morale: Criminalizing old-world nostalgia; enforcing joyful compliance; hosting the apocalyptic melodrama contests.

4. Minirenewal

Elemental Reclamation: Identity reset, surname striking, and the sublime honor of returning physical form to the compost vat.

5. Minilight

Luminescence & Grid: Rolling blackouts, kilowatt rationing, and forced bicycle generator labor to power the DJ booth.

6. Miniflow

Hydrological Purity: Moisture metering, graywater audits, and Peetopia biological fluid recovery enforcement.

7. Minibreath

Atmospheric Rectitude: Human breathing quotas, respiratory rhythm pacing, and carbon exhalation taxation.

8. Minishelter

Domestic Confinement: 15-minute perimeter enforcement, corridor curfews, and communal thermal compression.

9. Miniservice

Restorative Labor: Penal dishwashing (“greenwashing”), scullery shifts, and perpetual decontamination squads.

10. Minithrift

Material Confiscation: Seizure of petroleum fabrics, plastic trinkets, and unauthorized personal possessions.
