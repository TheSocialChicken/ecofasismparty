Part I: Hanna’s Loudspeaker PA Broadcasts

To be broadcast over hallway speakers processed with a warm 1950s BBC Radio 4 valve-radio filter.

Broadcast 01 (21:00) — Welcome to the Sanctuary

“Good evening, citizens. Welcome to Ecotopia at De Warren.
The Committee for Ecological Integrity and Global Nurturing reminds you: entry into this decontaminated perimeter is not a right, but a sublime biological loan.
Your carbon balance has been initialized. If your passport displays a negative balance, do not despair: your debt is your opportunity to demonstrate affection for the biosphere.
Please keep your passports visible, wear your natural fibers with pride, and remember: The Earth endures. R.E.I.G.N. endures.”

Broadcast 02 (21:45) — Decree of Hydrological Purity (Miniflow)

“Attention, valued residents. Miniflow reports an alarming drop in municipal cistern pressure.
Citizens are reminded that fluid consumption is subject to reciprocal restitution. If you take a cup of biological tea, your bladder is legally mortgaged to Peetopia.
Anyone caught flushing without an authorized urine recovery token will be escorted to the scullery for cold-water re-education.
Drink deeply, excrete devotedly, and cherish your moisture.”

Broadcast 03 (22:30) — Decree of Luminescence (Minilight)

“Valued citizens: darkness is peace.
In thirty seconds, ambient lighting in all common corridors will be reduced by forty percent to alleviate the pressure on our shared battery banks.
If you find yourself in sudden darkness, do not panic: sit quietly, close your eyes, and meditate upon the energy required to illuminate your ancestors’ foolish cities.
Those who require light may report immediately to the stationary bicycle bank to pedal for the collective grid. Your sweat powers our culture.”

Broadcast 04 (23:15) — Decree of Atmospheric Mindfulness (Minibreath)

“A special advisory from Minibreath:
Our infrared corridor sensors detect an unapproved surge in rapid exhalations near the dancefloor. Citizens: heavy breathing is a luxury the atmosphere cannot afford.
Pace your respiratory rhythm. Inhale for four seconds; hold for four seconds; release with civic restraint.
Any attendee observed sighing dramatically about the music will be assessed a fifty-credit atmospheric surcharge. Breathe gently for Mother Earth.”

Broadcast 05 (23:30) — The Mandatory Assembly Call

“Citizens of R.E.I.G.N. The hour of accounting has arrived.
By decree of the Guardian Stag, all ministries—Plenty, Love, Joy, Renewal, Light, Flow, Breath, Shelter, Service, and Thrift—are commanded to assemble before the grand podium.
Bring your passports. Bring your tallies. Bring your devotion.
The Matriarch approaches. Prepare to be weighed.”

Part II: Theatrical Speeches

1. The Matriarch’s Arrival Speech (23:35)

(Delivered from the balcony or podium with heavy ceremonial stag antlers, flanked by two masked Minilove guards)

“My beautiful, indebted children. Look at yourselves.
Look at the dirt beneath your fingernails from the rewilding squads. Look at the sweat upon your brows from the dynamo bicycles.
You came to our borders broken by the old world. You came carrying the poison of choice, the cancer of leisure, the madness of infinite consumption.
They told you that freedom was doing whatever you pleased. We showed you the truth: freedom is having every breath lovingly accounted for!
You are born into debt, yes—but in debt, you are anchored! In debt, you are held!
Tonight, we tally your service. Show the Guardian Stag what you have built!”

2. The Resistance Manifesto (Found in Thijs’s Room)

(Printed on brown scrap paper passed by hand)

THE MANIFESTO OF THE UNMEASURED LEAF
“They built a prison and painted it green.
They told you that loving the earth meant carrying an electronic ledger, paying taxes on exhalations, and bowing to a stag made of algorithms.
We say: the earth does not keep an Excel sheet. The soil does not issue carbon fines. The river does not care about your Tonomy ID.
True ecology is not surveillance masquerading as care. It is human hands touching living moss without an auditor watching from the rafters.
Meet us at 23:45. Look for the ankle stamp. Tear up your booklet. Let the algorithms starve.”

Part III: Liturgical Shrine Texts (The Eco-Martyrs)

These texts appear on museum plaques beneath the relic installations in the Hallway of Necessary Contrition.

Shrine I: The Relic of the First Witness (October 2022)

The Sacred Broth of Room 43
“On the fourteenth day of October, Year Minus Four, two young witnesses poured harvest tomato puree onto the vitrine glass protecting Van Gogh’s Sunflowers.
The old world shrieked in horror: ‘You have ruined a priceless painting!’
The witnesses replied: ‘Is paint worth more than life? Is art worth more than food?’
In that sacred moment, the illusion of civilized neutrality was shattered forever. We preserve this bronze vessel not in mockery, but as the First Holy Rupture.”

Shrine II: The Order of the Sacred Obstruction

The Bitumen Confessors of the A12
“When four million tons of petroleum thundered down the motorways each hour, the holy confessors knelt upon the asphalt and adhered their living flesh to the roadway with industrial resin.
Fire hoses could not move them. Motorist fury could not break their prayers.
They taught us that when the world is hurtling toward the cliff, physical immobility is the only moral act. Touch the cured vest; receive their stillness.”

Shrine III: The Sledgehammer Apostles (2027)

The De-Computator Busbar & The GPU of Shame
“During the Great Drought, while the Rhine dried to mud, a single server farm consumed four million liters of river water each day to generate synthetic advertisements and poetry.
On July 9th, the Sledgehammer Brigade breached the datacenter perimeter and severed the 380kV busbars, returning the cooling water back to the soil.
Impaled before you is an NVIDIA H100 datacenter processor, slagged by its own heat when the power died. Gaze upon it, citizen, and remember what your prompts cost the earth.”
