Ration Token Economy

The bar operates on ration tokens in true Republic style. Tokens are available at the Citizen Processing desk. One token = one drink. Additional tokens may be obtained by:

  • Successfully completing a Tribunal Re-enactment
  • Identifying contraband in the Black Market Corner
  • Denouncing a fellow citizen to a Bureau Official (theatrical only — no actual consequences unless they’re your mate and the bit goes on too long)
  • Correctly reciting a Covenant clause from memory
  • Providing an explanation for your pre-Republic carbon crimes that satisfies the Confession Booth operative

The Tribunal — Participation Guide

The Extinction Tribunal will convene at intervals throughout the evening. Citizens may be called as defendant or prosecutor. The charges are fictional. The dramatic stakes are real.

Sample Charges: - “The defendant owned a Land Rover and used it for the school run” - “The defendant flew to Ibiza four times in a single year and called it ‘necessary’” - “The defendant lobbied against cycle lane expansion in their local council” - “The defendant kept using plastic bags even after they cost five pence”

Defence options: - Ignorance (weak, but traditional) - Necessary economic activity (the Republic has heard this one) - Personal carbon offsets (the Tribunal will laugh) - Throwing a co-conspirator under the rewilding tractor

The Carbon Confession Booth

Unburden yourself. Tell the Booth Operative your greatest pre-Republic ecological sin. They will assess your carbon guilt on a scale of 1 (left the tap running) to 10 (owned an SUV with a personalised plate).

High scores receive a symbolic sentence — typically some form of theatrical penance performed in front of whoever is nearby.

All confessions are treated with the full gravity they deserve.

Getting There — Citizens Are Reminded

The Emerald Republic has abolished private vehicles. We note that it has not actually done this, as the Republic is fictional. Nevertheless, the organisers prefer you:

  • Walk / cycle / take the train / share a vehicle
  • Not show up in a diesel SUV and expect ideological immunity because you’re wearing the right costume

This is partly carbon guilt and partly just parking.

Tonight is satire. Tonight is a party. Tonight we play at the world that almost was — or might yet be — and we laugh at it and we dance in it and we go home at the end.

The point of dystopian fiction is to look at where the logic leads. The eco-fascism thesis starts with “what if they were actually right about the emergency?” and then asks “and what if that made them stop caring about how they fixed it?” That’s the bit. That’s the horror. Not that they were wrong about the planet. That they might have been right, and done this anyway.

Think about it. Or don’t. There’s a Confession Booth and a Dance Floor and it’s Year II and the earth is, somehow, still here.
