Dress Code & Sanctuary Safety

  • Approved Aesthetic: Dystopian utility chic, rewilding workwear, earth tones (moss green, soot black, soil brown), and natural biological fibers. Synthetic fast-fashion fabrics are strictly mocked.
  • The Universal Safe-Word (“OZONE”): Speaking “OZONE” immediately pauses all roleplay. All wardens, performers, and participants must drop character, step back, and assist you.
  • Observer Wristbands: Guests who wish to enjoy the music, cocktails, and dancing without engaging in interrogation, carbon audits, or chores may request an Observer Wristband at the door.
  • The Sanctuary Safe Zone: The library on the second floor is designated as a permanent, non-theatrical Safe Zone.
