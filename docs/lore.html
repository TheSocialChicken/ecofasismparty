The Chronicle of the Four Epochs

An Official History of R.E.I.G.N. — Ministry of Ecological Harmony, Year Zero

◆ HISTORY IS COMPILED BY THE STEWARDS ◆ THE SILICON MIND BOILED THE RIVERS ◆ THE FALLOUT UNITED THE SURVIVORS ◆ REMEMBER: WHAT CAME BEFORE WAS NOT LIVING — IT WAS BURNING ◆

What follows is the Canonical Chronicle of our salvation. Alternative versions exist only in the corrupted memories of unregistered scavengers. In Ecotopia, history is not a debate—it is an unbroken chain of ecological necessity.

The Four Historical Epochs

Epoch I — The Great Acceleration & The Compute Wars (~2025–2045)

In the final convulsions of the old economy, multinational artificial intelligence conglomerates devoured Europe’s electrical grid. Hyperscale server clusters demanded constant gigawatt baseloads, leading tech oligarchs to acquire dedicated nuclear reactors and freshwater watersheds to cool computing clusters. While ordinary citizens endured rolling green austerity blackouts and soaring tariffs, silicon chips burned the planet for algorithmic optimization. It was the age of digital excess, frantic automation, and atmospheric exhaustion.

Epoch II — The Great Withering & The Long Collapse (~2045–2080)

Humanity did not perish in a single evening. Over four grueling decades of cascading agricultural failures, thermal river collapses, and nuclear-containment breaches during extreme European heatwaves, 95% of human civilization withered away. Food distribution collapsed, cities emptied, and the surviving corporate conglomerates fused with militant ecological sects to seize the remaining habitable biomes.

Epoch III — The Age of the Ledger (~2080–Present)

Generations have now passed. The pre-collapse world of unmetered consumption, private property, and individual liberty has faded into distant, forbidden mythology. Surviving technocrats and ecologist-theocrats forged R.E.I.G.N. (Regime for Ecological Integrity and Global Nurturing). They established a programmable central-bank ledger grounded in one undeniable spiritual and physical truth: Every human being is born into Inherited Biological Sin (Carbon Debt). From your first infant breath, your existence is minted with a negative balance—compensating the living biosphere for the sins of old humanity, as well as your gestation, amniotic fluid, and lifetime oxygen demand.

Epoch IV — Ecotopia at Sanctuary De Warren

The decontaminated showcase sanctuary established at De Warren in Amsterdam. Here, selected applicants from the scorched borderlands arrive to confess their past complicity with fossil fuel culture, surrender their individualistic identities, and work down their inherited balances through joyful collective labor under the watchful benevolence of the Guardian Stag.

The Hagiography of the Eco-Martyrs

R.E.I.G.N. does not view early direct-action climate protest with irony. The regime canonizes these activists as The Holy Confessors of the Biological Pentecost—the early saints who recognized that human civilization was hurtling toward suicide:

The Apostles of the First Witness

Room 43 (October 2022): The two young confessors who poured harvest tomato puree across the glass of Van Gogh’s Sunflowers. To R.E.I.G.N., this was the First Sacred Rupture—the moment when human beings were forced to confront whether dead museum paint was dearer than the living soil.

The Order of the Sacred Obstruction

The Bitumen Confessors of the A12: The martyrs who adhered their flesh with industrial polymer to highway asphalt in the face of water cannons. Their voluntary physical immobility mirrors the ancient desert hermits who refused to participate in an insane empire.

The Sledgehammer Apostles

The De-Computator Brigades (2027): The militants who breached datacenter perimeters during the great drought and severed the 380kV high-voltage lines, cutting power to runaway server clusters to return cooling water back to the Rhine.

Guests entering De Warren will pass through the Hallway of Necessary Contrition, viewing the venerated relics of the pre-collapse resistance:

  • The Shroud of the High-Vis Tabard: A salt-cured, frayed fluorescent orange safety vest preserved under museum vitrine glass like the Shroud of Turin.
  • The GPU of Shame: A melted, slagged NVIDIA H100 datacenter processing board impaled upon a rusted length of construction rebar.
  • Sanctum Vasculum: The gilded bronze vessel commemorating the sacred puree of Room 43.
