Relic of the First Witness (October 2022)
"On the fourteenth day of October, Year Minus Four, two young witnesses poured harvest tomato puree onto the vitrine glass protecting Van Gogh’s Sunflowers. In that sacred moment, the illusion of civilized neutrality was shattered forever. We preserve this bronze vessel not in mockery, but as the First Holy Rupture."

"When four million tons of petroleum thundered down the motorways each hour, the holy confessors knelt upon the asphalt and adhered their living flesh to the roadway with industrial resin. Touch the cured vest; receive their stillness."
