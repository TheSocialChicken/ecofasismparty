1. CORE PREMISE & NARRATIVE
The European Wasteland & Sanctuary De Warren
The outside world suffered civilizational collapse. Somewhere in the ruined, scorched wasteland of Europe, word has spread of one place that still functions: Ecotopia at De Warren, the last sustainable sanctuary.

What arriving citizens discover is that Ecotopia is a totalitarian ecological regime governed by R.E.I.G.N. (Regime for Ecological Integrity and Global Nurturing). Corporations and ecologist-theocrats have fused into a paternalistic lockdown state.

Tonally, it is George Orwell's 1984, but the enemy is not a foreign superpower—the enemy is ecological collapse.
INHERITED BIOLOGICAL SIN: Every human is born into debt. You owe the earth for gestation, amniotic water, and lifetime oxygen. Servitude is your joyful repayment.
